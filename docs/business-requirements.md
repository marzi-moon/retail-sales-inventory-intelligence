
# Business Requirements Document

## 1. Project Overview

**Project:** Retail Sales & Inventory Intelligence Platform

**Client:** ABC Retail Group (Fictional)

**Objective:**
Design and implement a centralized Business Intelligence
solution to integrate retail sales, customer, product,
and inventory data and support data-driven decisions.

## 2. Business Problems

- Fragmented sales data across multiple systems.
- Inconsistent inventory information.
- Manual reporting processes.
- Limited visibility into product profitability.
- Lack of standardized business KPIs.

## 3. Data Sources

1. Point of Sales(POS) System
2. E-commerce System
3. Inventory Management System

Initial source format: CSV files.

## 4. Business Requirements

### Sales Analytics
- Analyze revenue trends.
- Monitor order volumes.
- Compare sales performance across stores and channels.

### Product Analytics
- Identify top-performing products.
- Analyze product profitability.
- Compare product categories.

### Customer Analytics
- Analyze purchasing behavior.
- Identify high-value customers.
- Monitor customer purchasing patterns.

### Inventory Analytics
- Monitor stock levels.
- Identify stock shortages and excess inventory.
- Calculate inventory turnover.

### Management Reporting
- Provide consolidated business KPIs.
- Enable analysis by date, product, customer, store, and channel.

## 5. Initial KPIs

- Total Revenue
- Gross Profit
- Gross Margin %
- Total Orders
- Average Order Value
- Inventory Turnover
- Stockout Rate
- Days of Inventory

## 6. Expected Deliverables

- SQL Server database architecture.
- Staging database and data warehouse.
- Dimensional data model.
- SSIS ETL packages.
- Data quality and reconciliation processes.
- SQL stored procedures.
- Power BI dashboards.
- Technical and business documentation.
